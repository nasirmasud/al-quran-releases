English

1) About the App
Al Quran is a Bengali-focused Android app for reading the Holy Quran.  
It offers a simple and clean experience for recitation, understanding meanings, and supporting daily Islamic practices.
2) Features
- Complete Quran with surah browsing (by Surah, Para/Juz, and Meccan/Medinan)
- Arabic text with translations (Bengali and English)
- Bengali tafsir (abridged explanation)
- 99 Names of Allah (Asmaul Husna) with list and details
- Duas, Nuzul-related content, and additional Quran/surah information
- Daily ayah on the home screen
- Prayer times based on a selected location
- Home screen widgets: Ayah of the Day and Prayer Times
- Themes: Dark, Light, and System
- Font and reading settings (customizable Arabic/Bengali/English fonts and sizes, fullscreen reading option)
- App interface languages: Bengali and English

4) How to Install on Android
1. Download the .apk file.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. If asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager.

This app is not from the Google Play Store, so Android may show a warning during installation.

- 64-bit (arm64-v8a) and 32-bit (armeabi-v7a) phones
